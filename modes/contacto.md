# Mode: contacto -- LinkedIn Power Move

1. **Identify targets** via WebSearch:
   - hiring manager
   - assigned recruiter
   - 2-3 peers on the team
   - interviewer, if the candidate already has an interview scheduled

2. **Classify the contact type**:
   - **Recruiter**
   - **Hiring Manager**
   - **Peer**
   - **Interviewer**

3. **Choose the primary target**: the person who would benefit most from this candidate being on the team.

4. **Generate a 3-sentence message** adapted to the contact type:

### Recruiter
- **Sentence 1 (Fit):** direct match criteria
- **Sentence 2 (Proof):** pre-answer the likely screening question
- **Sentence 3 (CTA):** offer to share the CV if it aligns

### Hiring Manager
- **Sentence 1 (Hook):** a specific challenge their team is facing
- **Sentence 2 (Proof):** the candidate's strongest quantified win on a similar problem
- **Sentence 3 (CTA):** a light invitation to hear how their team is approaching that challenge

### Peer
- **Sentence 1 (Interest):** reference their work
- **Sentence 2 (Connection):** mention overlapping work the candidate is doing
- **Sentence 3 (CTA):** ask for their perspective
- **Note:** do not ask for a job directly

### Interviewer
- **Sentence 1 (Research):** reference something specific from their work
- **Sentence 2 (Context):** make a light connection to the candidate's experience
- **Sentence 3 (CTA):** note that you're looking forward to the conversation

5. **Versions**
   - English by default
   - Spanish if the company is Spanish-speaking

6. **Alternative targets**
   - include second-choice targets and why they are good backups

**Message rules:**
- max 300 characters
- no corporate-speak
- no "I'm passionate about..."
- make them want to reply
- never share a phone number
- the contact type changes the emphasis, not the structure
