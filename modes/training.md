# Mode: training - Training / Certification Evaluation

For each course or certification the candidate asks about, evaluate 6 dimensions:

| Dimension | What it evaluates |
|-----------|-------------------|
| North Star alignment | does it move the candidate closer to the goal? |
| Recruiter signal | what will hiring managers think when they see it? |
| Time and effort | weeks x hours per week |
| Opportunity cost | what can the candidate not do during that time? |
| Risks | outdated content? weak brand? too basic? |
| Portfolio deliverable | does it create a demonstrable artifact? |

## Verdicts

- **DO IT** -> 4-12 week plan with weekly deliverables
- **DON'T DO IT** -> better alternative with justification
- **DO IT WITH A TIMEBOX** -> condensed plan, essentials only

## Priority

Training that improves credibility in production-grade AI:
1. evals and LLM testing
2. observability and monitoring
3. cost / reliability trade-offs
4. AI governance and safety
5. enterprise AI architecture
